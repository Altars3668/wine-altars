== 序列化：命名空间声明在祖先上
  appv:Extensions: <appv:Extensions xmlns:appv="urn:appv"><appv:Extension Id="x"/><Plain xmlns="urn:default"/></appv:Extensions>
  appv:Extension: <appv:Extension xmlns:appv="urn:appv" Id="x"/>
  Plain（默认命名空间）: <Plain xmlns="urn:default"/>
